How to Choose: 5 Questions Before You Pay

  • What volume will AI actually handle? Price per minute looks cheap until your after-hours queue doubles. Model your real monthly call minutes or load count first.
  • What happens on a bad transcription? Ask each vendor exactly how escalation to a human works — timeout rules, confidence thresholds, and who gets paged.
  • Can you export your data? Call transcripts and decision logs are your training and QA asset. Avoid lock-in.
  • Is it compliant for your jurisdiction? Emergency-adjacent use has rules; recording consent laws differ by state. Get this in writing from the vendor.
  • Does it start on non-emergency lines? The sane rollout is overflow/administrative first, emergency lines never fully automated. If a vendor promises otherwise, walk away.

FAQ

What is AI dispatch software? Software that uses speech recognition and language models to handle dispatch work — answering calls, collecting details, classifying priority, coordinating drivers or units — with humans keeping final authority.

How much does it cost? Voice-AI platforms usually bill per minute; trucking products per seat or vehicle per month; legacy suites by enterprise contract. Compare at your real volume, not the headline price.

Can it fully replace a dispatcher? No. See our deeper answer in Will AI Replace 911 Dispatchers? — the realistic model is supervision, not replacement.
